# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in `linkchecker-py` welcoming, respectful, and productive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarity when needed.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and its impact.
- Respect differing experience levels and communication styles.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would make the project unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile. Reports will be handled with care and discretion.
